Reading behavior

Privacy by design

  • Unfold observes selections locally. Nothing is sent until you choose Grow, Shrink or Explain.
  • Only the selected passage and a limited surrounding context are sent to Pollinations for that one transformation.
  • Your API key lives in the extension background. Content scripts and web pages cannot read it.
  • Generated passages are visibly marked, collapsible and removable. “Restore page” removes them and reveals originals.
  • Unfold does not run inside text fields and does not include analytics.

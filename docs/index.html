Why stretchtext waited fifty years

In 1967, Ted Nelson imagined stretchtext: prose that could grow where a reader wanted more detail and shrink back where they did not. The idea was elegant, but writing every level of every passage by hand made it impractical for ordinary documents.

Generated text changes the economics. A reader no longer needs the author to predict every question in advance; the missing layer can be produced at the moment of curiosity, while the original remains available for checking.

Install

Load Unfold in two minutes

Chrome / Edge / Brave

  1. Download and unzip unfold-stretchtext-v0.1.0.zip.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or the equivalent extensions page).
  3. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Open Unfold Settings and connect Pollinations.

Firefox

  1. Download and unzip the release.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Choose Load Temporary Add-on and select manifest.json.
  4. Open Unfold Settings and connect Pollinations. Temporary add-ons are removed when Firefox restarts.

Privacy

Nothing leaves the page until you ask.

  • Selection-first: Unfold observes selections locally and sends text only after Grow, Shrink or Explain.
  • Limited payload: only the selected passage and a bounded surrounding context are sent to Pollinations.
  • Background-only key: web pages and content scripts never receive your API key.
  • Reversible: inserted passages are marked and removable; Restore reveals original text.
  • No analytics: Unfold includes no tracking or third-party analytics.

Questions

Who pays for generation?

The reader. Unfold uses Pollinations BYOP/Device Flow or a key the reader supplies, so each transformation is charged to that reader's Pollen balance.

Does Unfold rewrite the whole page automatically?

No. That would be expensive and invasive. It works passage by passage, at the exact point where the reader asks for more or less.

What if the generated layer is wrong?

It is visibly marked, collapsible and removable. Shrink keeps the original one click away, and Unfold prompts models not to invent citations or facts unsupported by the supplied context.

Which model does it use?

The default is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, with GPT-5.4 Nano and GLM-5.3 Flash presets plus a custom Pollinations model ID field.
